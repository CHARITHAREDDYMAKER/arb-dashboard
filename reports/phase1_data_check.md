## 6. Gaps and things to know

1. **Warehouse storage, assaying and handling rates are missing.** NCCL publishes them, but only on a page behind a browser check. Open the page in a normal browser, copy the Unjha (jeera) and Jodhpur (guar seed) rates into `config/carry_costs.toml`.
2. **Guar seed basis centre is Jodhpur, not Bikaner.** Bikaner is an additional delivery centre at a discount. The spot price in the bhavcopy is therefore the Jodhpur price.
3. **The spot price has not been cross-checked against the NCDEX spot page**, because that page is behind the browser check. Compare two or three dates by hand in a browser before relying on it.
4. **Far-month contracts are listed but barely trade.** Many far expiries show zero volume and zero open interest; their settlement price looks like an exchange-computed theoretical value, not a traded one. Term-structure and rollover charts should mark or drop contracts with no open interest.
5. **Taxes and smaller fees are unverified** (SEBI fee, stamp duty, CTT exemption, GST treatment). They are listed as `to_verify` in the config with no values used yet.
6. **FBIL public data lags by about 9 days.** Fill the most recent days from the last available value or the RBI auction yield.
7. **NCDEX throttles bursts.** After roughly ten quick requests it drops connections for a minute. The 12-month download (about 250 requests) should run with a 5-second delay and retries: roughly 25 minutes, run once and cached.
8. **Two expiry columns.** `XpryDt` is the scheduled date and `FininstrmActlXpryDt` the actual one; they differ when the scheduled date is a holiday. We use the actual date.

**MCX copper fallback: not needed.**
